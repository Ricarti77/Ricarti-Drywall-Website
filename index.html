    Nobody walks into a finished room and admires the drywall. They notice the light from the window, the colour of the paint, the height of the ceiling. If they notice the wall, something has gone wrong: a joint catching the afternoon sun, a ridge along the ceiling, a corner that is not quite straight.

    On Kent we boarded, taped and finished the whole house, from the bedrooms to the long stair hall where light runs down the wall from above. Stair halls are where shortcuts show first. Every joint got its coats, every screw was set and filled, and every wall was checked at the end with a work light held flat to the surface, the way low winter sun will hit it.

    Then the painters came, and the walls disappeared into the rooms. Which is exactly the point.

    Field notes · under the board

    The best work on a job is the work you never see.

    Before a single sheet goes up, the wall is insulated, sealed and inspected. Get this layer right and the house is warmer, quieter and drier for decades. Get it wrong and no amount of finishing will fix it.

    Warmth
    Closed-cell foam at rim joists and sloped ceilings, batts in the walls
    Quiet
    Mineral wool, resilient channel and sealed penetrations
    Dry
    A continuous air and vapour barrier, checked before it is covered

    Both. We take projects directly from homeowners who are building or renovating, and we work as the insulation and drywall trade for builders and general contractors on detached homes, semis and townhouses.

    Which areas do you serve?+

    Are you insured and registered with the WSIB?+

    Yes. We carry [$X million] in liability insurance and are registered with the WSIB. Builders receive our clearance certificate and certificate of insurance before work starts.

    What is the difference between a Level 4 and a Level 5 finish?+

    Level 4 is three coats over the joints and screws, and suits most painted rooms. Level 5 adds a skim coat over the entire wall or ceiling, so joints do not show under raking light, gloss paint or dark colours.

    How long does drywall take on a new house?+
