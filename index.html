GUIDE

Level 4 or Level 5? Know what you are paying for.

Drywall finish is graded from Level 0, board only, to Level 5. Most painted homes are finished to Level 4. Level 5 adds a thin skim coat over the whole surface, and it is what hides joints under raking light, on tall walls, and under dark or glossy paint. We will tell you which rooms need it, and which do not.

What is the difference between a Level 4 and a Level 5 finish?+

Level 4 is three coats over the joints and screws, and suits most painted rooms. Level 5 adds a skim coat over the entire wall or ceiling, so joints do not show under raking light, gloss paint or dark colours.
