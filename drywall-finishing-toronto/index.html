Drywall taping and finishing, to the level the room needs.

THE SHORT ANSWER

We finish painted walls to Level 4 as standard and recommend a Level 5 skim coat where strong side light, tall walls, or dark or glossy paint would show the joints. You get the finish level for every room in writing before we start.

The six finish levels, in plain words.

The drywall industry grades finish from Level 0 to Level 5. Naming the level in your contract is the simplest way to make sure "paint-ready" means the same thing to you and to us.

LEVELWHAT IS DONEWHERE IT IS USED
0Board hung. No tape or compound.Temporary walls
1Tape set in compound at the joints.Hidden areas, above ceilings
2Tape plus one coat over joints and screws.Garages, under tile
3A second coat over joints and screws.Under heavy texture
4A third coat, sanded smooth.Most painted rooms: flat and eggshell
5Level 4, then a skim coat over the entire surface.Raking light, tall walls, curved walls, gloss or dark paint

When Level 5 is worth paying for.

Light along the wall

Floor-to-ceiling windows, skylights and pot lights close to a wall throw shadows across every ridge.

Tall and curved walls

Double-height foyers and stair walls are seen from far away and at an angle.

Dark or glossy paint

Sheen and deep colours show the difference between paper and compound.

Where it is not

Closets, utility rooms and most bedrooms in flat paint. We will tell you, room by room.

Finishing questions.

How much more does Level 5 cost than Level 4?

It adds a full skim coat and the labour to sand it, so expect [your typical range, e.g. X–Y% more] on the finishing line of the quote. Doing it only where the light demands it keeps the cost down.

Can I tell the finish level by looking?

Hold a bright light flat against the wall, or look along it toward a window. Joints and screw heads that disappear head-on often show at that angle. It is how we check our own work.

Do you finish drywall someone else hung?

Yes, after we look at it. Board hung badly costs more to finish well, and we will say so in the quote.
