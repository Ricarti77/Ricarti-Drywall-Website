    Service 03 · Level 4 & Level 5 Finishing

    Surfaces Ready for the Light.

    Finishing is where a wall becomes beautiful or ordinary. We tape, coat and sand to the level each room needs, with a full skim coat where light and paint demand it.

    Request an EstimateBuilders — Send Your Drawings
    IN SHORTRicarti Drywall finishes painted walls to Level 4 as standard and recommends Level 5, a full skim coat, where strong side light, tall walls, or dark or glossy paint would show the joints. We also remove popcorn ceilings, create smooth ceiling finishes and repair drywall and plaster. The finish level for each room is written into the estimate.

    The service

    Level 4 or Level 5?

    Level 4 is three coats of joint compound over taped joints and fasteners, sanded smooth. It suits most painted rooms with flat or eggshell paint.

    Level 5 adds a thin skim coat over the entire surface. It evens out the difference between paper and compound, so joints do not show under raking light, on tall walls, or beneath dark or glossy paint. We recommend it only where it pays, and say so room by room.

    The finish levels

    The six finish levels, in plain words.

    The industry grades drywall finish from Level 0 to Level 5. Naming the level in the contract makes sure “paint-ready” means the same thing to you and to us.

    LevelWhat is doneWhere it is used
    0Board hung. No tape or compound.Temporary walls
    1Tape set in compound at joints.Hidden areas, above ceilings
    2Tape plus one coat over joints and screws.Garages, under tile
    3A second coat over joints and screws.Under heavy texture
    4A third coat, sanded smooth.Most painted rooms: flat and eggshell paint
    5Level 4 plus a skim coat over the whole surface.Raking light, tall walls, curved walls, glossy or dark paint

    Materials and systems

    The right system for the job.

    Paper and mesh joint tape
    Chosen for each joint type, embedded in compound for a strong, crack-resistant seam.
    Joint compounds
    Setting-type and ready-mix compounds used where each performs best.
    Corner beads
    Paper-faced, metal or vinyl beads for straight, durable corners.
    Skim coat
    A thin coat over the entire surface for Level 5 finishes.
    Primer-ready surfaces
    Sanded, cleaned and checked under a work light before paint.

    Questions

    Frequently asked.

    How much more does Level 5 cost than Level 4?+

    Level 5 adds a full coat of compound and the labour to sand it, so it costs more on the finishing line. Using it only where the light demands it keeps the cost down. Your estimate shows the finish level for each room.

    Can I tell the finish level by looking?+

    Hold a bright light flat against the wall, or look along it toward a window. Joints and screw heads that disappear face-on often show at that angle. It is how we check our own work.

    Can you remove popcorn ceilings?+

    Yes. Textured ceilings can be removed and refinished smooth, or covered with new drywall. Ceilings installed before the 1990s may contain asbestos. In Ontario, asbestos on construction projects is regulated under Ontario Regulation 278/05, which requires material that may contain asbestos to be identified before it is disturbed. We have the texture tested before any removal and plan the work around the result.

    Do you finish drywall hung by another company?+

    Yes, after we have looked at it. Poorly hung board costs more to finish well, and we will say so in the estimate.
