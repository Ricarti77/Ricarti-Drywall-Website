    Service 05 · Acoustic & Soundproofing

    Quiet, by Design.

    A quieter room comes from the right combination of mass, decoupling, absorption and sealing. We build sound-control assemblies into walls and ceilings, and tell you honestly what each upgrade can do.

    Request an EstimateBuilders — Send Your Drawings
    IN SHORTRicarti Drywall builds sound-control walls and ceilings for bedrooms, bathrooms, home offices, entertainment rooms and selected commercial spaces across Toronto and the GTA, using mineral wool and acoustic batts such as ROCKWOOL Safe’n’Sound and Owens Corning QuietZone, SONOpan panels, resilient channel and multi-layer drywall. Performance depends on the full assembly, including how it is sealed.

    The service

    Absorption or isolation?

    Sound absorption reduces echo and reverberation inside a room. Acoustic panels and soft finishes do this, but they do little to stop sound passing through a wall.

    Sound isolation stops sound travelling from one space to another. It comes from the wall or ceiling assembly itself: mass, decoupling, absorptive insulation in the cavity, and airtight sealing. Soundproofing a room is mostly about isolation, and that is what we build.

    Materials and systems

    The right system for the job.

    Product names are used to describe the systems we install. Ricarti Drywall has no official partnership or distribution agreement with these manufacturers.

    ROCKWOOL Safe’n’Sound
    Stone wool batts for interior walls and floors that add sound absorption in the cavity and fire resistance.
    Owens Corning QuietZone
    Acoustic fibreglass batts designed to reduce noise transfer through interior walls, floors and ceilings.
    SONOpan panels
    Soundproofing panels made from recycled wood fibre, installed under drywall as part of a sound-control assembly.
    Resilient channel
    Metal channel that decouples drywall from the framing, reducing vibration transfer.
    Multi-layer drywall
    Additional layers of board to add mass to walls and ceilings.
    Acoustic sealant
    Flexible sealant at perimeters, outlets and penetrations, where sound would otherwise leak.

    Questions

    Frequently asked.

    What is the difference between sound absorption and sound isolation?+

    Absorption reduces echo inside a room; isolation stops sound passing between rooms. Soundproofing a wall or ceiling is mostly about isolation, which comes from mass, decoupling, cavity insulation and sealing.

    What STC rating will my wall achieve?+

    STC ratings come from laboratory tests of complete, specified assemblies. We build assemblies as specified, but we do not promise a particular rating unless the exact tested assembly is being built. Real-world results also depend on flanking paths, doors and openings.

    Can you soundproof an existing room?+

    Yes. Common upgrades add acoustic insulation, resilient channel or SONOpan and an extra layer of drywall, with every gap sealed. Read our soundproofing guide.

    Does insulation alone soundproof a wall?+

    It helps, but on its own the improvement is modest. The best results combine insulation with added mass, decoupling and careful sealing.
