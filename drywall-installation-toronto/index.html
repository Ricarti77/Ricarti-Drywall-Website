    Service 01 · Drywall Systems

    Drywall, Installed with Intent.

    Every finish starts with how the board goes up. We plan layouts, joints and fastening so walls and ceilings are flat, solid and ready for an exceptional finish.

    Request an EstimateBuilders — Send Your Drawings
    IN SHORTRicarti Drywall installs drywall systems for custom homes, renovations and selected commercial interiors across Toronto, the GTA, Hamilton, Kitchener-Cambridge and Muskoka: walls, ceilings and bulkheads in standard, Type X fire-rated, moisture-resistant and tile-backer board, including multi-layer assemblies.

    The service

    Flat, solid and planned.

    Good drywall work is decided before the first sheet goes up. We review the framing, plan sheet layout to keep joints out of strong light, and choose the right board for each room: standard, fire-rated, moisture-resistant or tile backer.

    For builders, that means a clean, inspection-ready installation on schedule. For homeowners, it means walls and ceilings that look right for decades.

    Technical capabilities

    What we deliver.

    Materials and systems

    The right system for the job.

    Product names are used to describe the systems we install. Ricarti Drywall has no official partnership or distribution agreement with these manufacturers.

    Standard 1/2-inch drywall
    The everyday board for interior walls in most homes.
    5/8-inch Type X drywall
    Fire-rated board used where a fire-resistance rating is required, such as garage separations and walls between units.
    Moisture-resistant drywall
    For bathrooms, laundry rooms and basements where humidity is higher.
    DensShield tile backer
    A glass-mat gypsum backer for tiled walls in wet areas.
    Multi-layer assemblies
    Two or more layers of board for fire-rated or sound-rated walls and ceilings, built as specified.

    Questions

    Frequently asked.

    What thickness of drywall do you use?+

    Standard 1/2-inch board for most interior walls, and 5/8-inch Type X where a fire rating is required or where the drawings call for it. Ceilings and special assemblies follow the specification.

    Where is Type X drywall required?+

    Type X is used in fire-rated assemblies, for example between an attached garage and the house, or between units in semis and townhouses. The exact requirement comes from the drawings and the applicable building code.

    Do you install drywall in bathrooms?+

    Yes. We use moisture-resistant board on bathroom walls and ceilings, and DensShield or a similar tile backer behind tile where it is specified.

    Do you work on commercial projects?+

    We take on selected commercial interiors in addition to custom homes and residential projects. Send us the drawings to discuss your project.
