    Our process

    From drawings to paint-ready.

    Five clear stages, so you always know what happens next and who is responsible.

    1. 01

      Drawing & Scope Review

      We review your architectural drawings, energy specification and finish requirements, and confirm the scope with you before pricing.

    2. 02

      Detailed Estimate

      A written price per unit, per block or per project that names the insulation, board and finish levels, with inclusions and exclusions.

    3. 03

      Scheduling & Coordination

      Crews and start dates planned around your insulation-ready and board-ready dates, coordinated with your site supervisor and other trades.

    4. 04

      Professional Execution

      Insulation installed ready for inspection, then board, tape and finish by one coordinated crew.

    5. 05

      Quality Review & Completion

      A walkthrough with your site supervisor before handover, with any deficiencies listed and closed.

    Do you supply materials or labour only?+

    Can you run several houses at once?+

    Yes. Tell us your phasing and we will staff to it. [Confirm current capacity: number of houses at once.]

    Do you work on high-rise towers or commercial projects?+

    We do not work on high-rise towers. Our focus is custom homes and low-rise residential (detached homes, semis and townhouses), and we take on selected commercial projects.

    [Your terms: deposit, progress draws, invoicing schedule.] Statutory holdback under Ontario's Construction Act applies as usual.
