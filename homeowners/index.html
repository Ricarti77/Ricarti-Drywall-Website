IN SHORT

Ricarti Drywall insulates, boards and finishes custom homes, additions and basements for homeowners. You get a site visit, a written scope that names the finish level for each room, a clean site every day and a final walkthrough with you. Family-owned, since 2009.

What you can expect, in writing.

You deal with the owner

The person who quotes your job is the person responsible for it. You have one phone number from site visit to walkthrough.

A price you can compare

Your quote lists insulation type, board type, finish level by room, corner details, protection, clean-up and dates. Line by line, so you can compare it with any other quote.

A house you can live around

Floors and windows covered, dust controlled while sanding, debris bagged and the site swept at the end of every day.

Honest advice on finish

We recommend Level 5 only where light or paint will show the joints, and say plainly where Level 4 is enough.

A walkthrough before we leave

We check every wall with you, with a work light held flat to the surface, and fix what it shows before paint.

Projects we take on for homeowners.

New custom homes

Insulation through paint-ready walls, working alongside your builder or architect.

Additions

New rooms finished to match the walls and ceilings you already have.

Basements

Steel framing, insulation for warmth and sound, moisture-resistant board, bulkheads.

Repairs & older homes

Ceiling repairs, patching after other trades, and lath and plaster walls.

Quieter rooms

Sound insulation and sound-rated board for bedrooms, offices and media rooms.

What changes the price.

Every house is different, so we price after we have seen it. These are the things that move the number.

Size
Square footage of wall and ceiling, not just floor area
Height
Ceilings over 9 ft, foyers and stair walls need scaffolding or lifts
Finish level
Level 5 adds a full skim coat; we use it only where it pays
Insulation
Batts, blown-in or spray foam, and how much of each
Details
Bulkheads, curved walls, arches, recessed details
Access
Occupied home, stairs, parking and board delivery

Homeowners usually ask.

Can we stay in the house while you work?

Usually, yes. We seal off the rooms we are working in, cover floors and control sanding dust. For a full-house job we will tell you which days are noisiest.

Do you work with my builder or architect?

Yes. Many of our homeowner projects are custom builds where we coordinate with the general contractor, the architect and the inspector. You still get the scope and price in writing.

Do you take small jobs?

Yes: ceiling repairs, a single basement room, patching after a plumber or electrician. Small jobs get the same finish as large ones. [Minimum job size, if you have one.]
