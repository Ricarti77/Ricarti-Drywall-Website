    What affects your estimate

    Every home is different, so we price after we have seen it. These are the things that move the number.

    Size
    Square footage of wall and ceiling, not just floor area
    Height
    Ceilings over 9 ft, foyers and stair walls need scaffolding or lifts
    Finish level
    Level 5 adds a full skim coat; we use it only where it pays
    Insulation
    Batts, blown-in or spray foam, and how much of each
    Details
    Bulkheads, curved walls, arches, recessed details
    Access
    Occupied home, stairs, parking and board delivery

    How do I request an estimate?+

    Use the estimate form to send photos or plans, call (647) 482-1139 or email cris@ricartidrywall.ca. We will arrange a visit and give you a written estimate.

    Can you help improve my home's insulation?+

    Yes. We install batt, mineral wool, blown-in and spray foam insulation and seal air leaks, and we recommend which material belongs where in your home. Read our insulation guide.

    Can you remove popcorn ceilings?+

    Yes. Textured ceilings can be removed and refinished smooth, or covered with new drywall. Ceilings installed before the 1990s may contain asbestos, so the texture should be tested by a qualified lab before any work. If it tests positive, removal must follow Ontario's asbestos regulations.

    Can you help soundproof existing rooms?+

    Yes. Typical upgrades add mineral wool in the walls or ceiling, resilient channel and an extra layer of drywall, with acoustic sealant at every gap. Read our soundproofing guide.

    Do you work on occupied homes?+

    Yes. We seal off the rooms we are working in, cover floors and control sanding dust, and we let you know in advance which days will be noisiest.

    Which areas do you serve?+

    Toronto and the whole Greater Toronto Area, plus Hamilton, Kitchener–Cambridge and the Muskoka region. See where we work.
